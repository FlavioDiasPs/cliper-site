Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Cliper is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not collect data from the public or from other users.

What it accesses

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, Cliper receives an access token that lets it publish the owner's videos to that account. It uses that access only to publish the owner's own content.

Storage

The token is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not shared, sold or sent to anyone else. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok's account settings under security and permissions.

Third parties

Cliper sends content only to the platforms the owner chooses to publish on. It does not use advertising or analytics trackers.
